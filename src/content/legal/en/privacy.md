This page says exactly what happens to your data when you use the site. It is written to
be read rather than ticked off — if anything is unclear, write to me and I will rewrite it.

Short version: the only moment you hand me anything is when you fill in the contact form or
book a call. There are no tracking cookies, no profiling, no mailing list, and I sell
nothing to anybody.

## The contact form

When you submit the form you send me: your **name**, your **email address**, the **project
type** and **budget** you picked from the lists, the **message** you wrote and, optionally,
your current **website address**.

The message reaches me one of two ways, depending on how the site is configured at the
moment you use it:

- **Straight from your own email client.** The site opens a pre-filled email window and you
  press send. The data passes through nobody; it goes from you to me. You can tell this is
  what happened because your mail app opens.
- **Through Web3Forms.** A service that takes the form submission and emails it to me. At
  the moment of delivery, Web3Forms sees what you typed into the form. Their policy:
  [web3forms.com/privacy](https://web3forms.com/privacy).

Either way the message lands in my inbox and stays there for as long as it is useful to our
conversation or to a contract. I use it **only to reply to you** — it goes on no list, to
no one else, and into no advertising.

The form also carries a hidden field for bots (a honeypot). It holds nothing about you; it
exists only to cut down spam.

## Booking a call

The booking button loads **Cal.com** into a frame on the page, and only once you press it.
If you book, the details you fill in there — usually name, email and the slot you chose —
go to Cal.com and then to me, in my calendar. Their policy:
[cal.com/privacy](https://cal.com/privacy).

If you never press the button, Cal.com does not load at all and learns nothing about your
visit.

## Cookies

**The site sets no cookies.** Not for tracking, not for statistics, not even functional
ones — which is why there is no banner asking for your consent.

Two things are saved in your own browser through `localStorage`, and they **never leave
it**:

- **your chosen theme** (dark or light), so it is not reset on every visit;
- **the demo data** — the bookings and the basket on the demo pages. It is invented, it is
  yours, and you can clear it with the "Reset the demo data" button on each demo, or by
  clearing site data in your browser.

The Cal.com booking frame may set its own cookies, in their context, but only if you open
it.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on **Cloudflare**. Like any host, Cloudflare processes your IP address
and the technical details of the request in order to serve you the page and to block
attacks. I have no visitor list and I run no traffic analytics at this time.

## The audit tool

The site has a band where you can ask for a quick audit of a website. When it is switched
on, the address you type there is sent to **Google PageSpeed Insights**, which returns the
scores. **Only the address being analysed** is sent — nothing about you, no name, no email.

## Your rights

You have the right to know what data I hold about you, to correct it, to have it deleted,
to object to its use and to receive a copy. In practice, what I hold about you is the
emails you sent me.

Write to [alexdelcea1996@gmail.com](mailto:alexdelcea1996@gmail.com) and I will sort it
within 30 days at the latest — usually the same day. If you ask me to delete it, I delete
the conversation and confirm.

If something seems wrong and we cannot resolve it between us, you can complain to the
Romanian data protection authority, **ANSPDCP**,
[dataprotection.ro](https://www.dataprotection.ro).
