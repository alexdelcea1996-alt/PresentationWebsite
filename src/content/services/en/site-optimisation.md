## Three things costing you customers without you noticing

**Speed.** A site that takes five seconds loses a good share of visitors arriving by phone on mobile
data. They do not leave angry and they do not write to you — they simply never get to see the page.
It is the quietest possible loss.

**The mobile layout.** Not "looks a bit odd", but buttons you cannot hit with a thumb, text running
off the screen, forms impossible to complete. If most of your visitors are on mobile, a site that
only works on a desktop is working at half capacity.

**The technical SEO basics.** Missing or identical titles across every page, images without
descriptions, no sitemap, duplicate pages. Nothing spectacular — but exactly the things Google checks
automatically and that you can fully control.

## When I tell you not to fix it

Not every site deserves saving, and I would rather lose the job than take your money for something
that will not help you.

I recommend a new site when fixing costs more than half of a new build, when the theme is so old it
no longer receives security updates, or when the structure is so tangled that any small change risks
breaking something else.

In those cases I give you both figures — the cost to fix and the cost to rebuild — and let you
decide. No pressure and no invented deadlines.

## How it goes

You send me the address. Within 48 hours you get the written audit, with problems ordered by impact
and a price for each. You choose what I fix — you can take only the first item on the list, if that
is all that makes sense right now. I work on a copy, test, then move to live. At the end you get the
same set of measurements, so you see the difference in numbers.
