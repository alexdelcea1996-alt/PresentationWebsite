## Why most business websites bring in nothing

I have taken over enough badly built sites to see the pattern. Nearly all of them share the same
three problems, and none of the three is about how the site looks.

**They talk about the company, not the client.** "We are a team of professionals with over ten years
of experience" says nothing. The client has a problem and wants to know whether you solve it. The
copy has to start from their problem.

**They do not say clearly what the visitor should do.** With no obvious button, people read, nod and
close the tab. A good site has one next step, repeated calmly throughout.

**They load slowly on phones.** More than half of visitors arrive on mobile, on mobile data. If the
page takes five seconds, a good share of them never see it at all.

## How working together goes

We spend 30 minutes on what you do, who your clients are and what you want to achieve. You get a

I do not disappear after handover. The 60 days of support are not contract filler — they are the
